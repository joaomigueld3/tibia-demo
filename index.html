Rookgaard Controls & Guide
  • Walk: WASD / Arrow Keys, Q/E/Z/C (diagonals), or Left-Click ground tiles.
  • Attack: Click a monster in the viewport or Battle List (Esc cancels target).
  • Look / Inspect: Right-Click or Shift + Left-Click (or toggle Look).
  • Loot & Chests: Click an adjacent corpse/chest or press Space to quick-loot.
  • Backpack: Click Food to eat, Potions to drink, or Gear (like Short Sword) to equip.
  • Spells: F1 (exura) heals HP; F2 (exevo flam hur) blasts fire forward.
  • NPCs & Sewer: Say hi or heal near Monk Cipfried in the Temple. Step onto or click the Sewer Grate east of town to enter the Rat Sewer!
